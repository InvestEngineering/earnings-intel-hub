Granular Financial Driver Models

Rather than relying on simple extrapolation, this section dissects FEDFINA's financial forecast into granular underlying operational mechanics: Volume × Yield for Revenue, Operating Leverage & Branch Maturity for EBITDA, and Provisioning Collapse (<1.0% credit cost) for PAT.

📈 Revenue Driver Model
  • AUM Volume Compounding: Modeled at 22%–24% YoY (slightly trailing management guidance of 25% to build a margin of safety for regulatory gold LTV friction).
  • Blended Yield Stability: Anchored between 16.8% and 17.1%. Backed by co-lending partnerships (Federal Bank, SBI) providing off-balance sheet volume off-take.
  • Portfolio Mix Rotation: Gold loan share shifting subtly from 53% to ~50% due to RBI bullet-loan rules, offset by aggressive Small-Ticket LAP (ST-LAP) scaling.
⚙️ EBITDA Driver Model
  • Low Borrowing Cost Moat: Cost of Borrowing (COB) compressed to 8.6% (down 40 bps), maintaining a ~200 bps spread advantage over mid-tier peers via Federal Bank parentage (AA+ rating).
  • "Vyapar" Co-Location Synergy: 70 ST-LAP branches merged inside existing gold hubs, cutting real estate footprint costs in half and boosting EBITDA margins.
  • Branch Maturation Cycle: 148 branches added in FY26 hitting profitability thresholds as average AUM per branch swelled to ₹16.5 Crore.
🛡️ PAT Cascade & Risk Cleansing
  • Unsecured Lending Exit: High-risk Unsecured Business Loans (UBL) slashed to <2% of AUM, eliminating the primary source of historical default shocks.
  • Sub-1.0% Credit Cost Guarantee: Stage-3 Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR) fortified to 38.4%, ensuring provisions remain low (~₹18-24 Cr/qtr) while PPOP surges.
  • High Quality Core Earnings: Direct Assignment (DA) income slashed 89% in FY26 to ₹7 Cr. Over 95% of future PAT is pure recurring interest spread.
