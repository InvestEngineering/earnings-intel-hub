African duty-free access to the US expires Dec 2026. Non-renewal severely impairs the $120M Atraco strategic model.

Government Export Incentive Cuts (RoSCTL / RoDTEP)

DGFT cut RoDTEP by 50%. An equivalent RoSCTL cut would instantly destroy up to 1.75% of Gokaldas's operational margin bridge.

96.3% Promoter Equity Pledge

Extreme promoter pledge introduces acute liquidity risk if market volatility triggers margin calls.
