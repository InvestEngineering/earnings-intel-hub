Executive Summary: Amalgamation, Restructuring & Inflection

Viyash Scientific Ltd (NSE: VIYASH), formed via the landmark November 2025 amalgamation of Viyash Life Sciences and SeQuent Scientific, has transformed into a fully backward-integrated pharmaceutical enterprise spanning both animal and human health. The Q1 FY27 results confirm a powerful operational turning point. Driven by a 33% YoY surge in global formulations and 220 bps gross margin expansion, adjusted EBITDA expanded by 59.2% YoY to ₹204.70 Cr. With net debt falling to ₹86 Cr (0.1x EBITDA), the company is pivoting into scaled execution, margin expansion, and accelerated cash flow generation.

Catalyst 1 Q3 FY27 Onward

BioForLife Italia M&A

The pending €16.976M acquisition provides immediate access to 80% of Italian veterinary clinics. It establishes a direct distribution springboard into Europe's lucrative companion animal market, adding €9M+ baseline revenue scaling into FY28.

Catalyst 2 Q2 FY27 Immediate

API Segment Record Quarter

Following transient June destocking, management explicitly guided Q2 FY27 to be the strongest API quarter in corporate history. Backed by USFDA clearance for the Vizag facility, high-margin API exports are accelerating.

Catalyst 3 Q1 FY28 Alpha

Non-Cash ESOP & D&A Cliff

Non-cash ESOP charges drop from ~₹150 Cr in FY27 to ₹25-30 Cr in FY28. Coupled with expiring legacy intangible amortizations (~₹60 Cr benefit), pre-tax PAT will surge by +52.2% independent of sales growth.

The P&L Translation Engine: Unpacking the Non-Cash ESOP Cliff

While top-line revenue expands at ~15% CAGR, Net Profit (PAT) is set to surge by +52.2% in FY28. This decoupling is driven by two specific accounting step-downs below the EBITDA line: (1) The expiration of composite scheme non-cash ESOP charges dropping from ₹150 Cr to ₹30 Cr annually, and (2) The complete amortization of legacy acquisition intangibles in H2 FY27.
