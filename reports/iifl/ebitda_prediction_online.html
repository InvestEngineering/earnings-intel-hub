This interactive report presents an independent forward earnings model for IIFL Finance Ltd following the complete resolution of the Reserve Bank of India's (RBI) gold loan regulatory embargo. With the consolidated loan book permanently transformed toward ~90% secured retail assets (Gold & Housing Finance) and unsecured personal/MSME loans fully discontinued, credit costs are projected to drop dramatically from historical highs of 2.8%+ down to 1.5%–1.7%. This structural shift creates a powerful operating leverage cycle where PAT growth systematically outpaces top-line revenue expansion over the next 8 quarters.

Gold Loan Normalization

AUM doubled YoY to ₹58,406 Cr in Q1 FY27 (+11% QoQ). Higher global gold prices increase collateral value and lower Loss Given Default (LGD).

Project PACE AI Operating Leverage

Sequential fixed opex grew by only ~2% in Q1 despite massive AUM expansion. ML-based fraud detection and voice AI constrain Opex/AUM to 3.3%–3.4%.

Capital-Light Co-Lending

Co-lending AUM reached ₹14,674 Cr (+27% YoY), targeted for 40%–45% of total AUM. Structurally boosts fee income and drives ROE toward ~25%.

Contingent Tax Demands

Income tax demands of ₹963.39 Cr (housing sub) and ₹475.56 Cr (parent) present non-recurring downside risk, prudentally built into conservative PAT scenarios.

Strategic Earnings Analysis Q&A Synthesis

Direct answers to core institutional questions regarding IIFL Finance's forward earnings trajectory.

Revenue is expected to grow between 10.90% and 23.80% YoY through FY27, normalizing to ~13.5%–14.5% in FY28 as base effects stabilize. EBITDA (PPOP) will expand faster at 12.59% to 36.43% YoY in FY27 due to fixed cost dilution via Project PACE.

Q3 FY27 (Q+2E) marks the inflection peak with a forecasted YoY PAT growth of +54.58% (₹775 Cr v/s ₹501.35 Cr prior). This is driven by post-monsoon gold loan origination seasonality combined with normalized provisions.

This mathematical leverage is caused by plunging credit costs (falling from historical levels of ~2.8% to 1.5%–1.7%). As high-risk unsecured loans run off, less operating profit is absorbed by loan loss provisions, allowing a larger percentage of PPOP to flow straight to PAT.

Consensus models heavily focus on gold AUM recovery but underestimate the structural margin shift driven by Project PACE. Sequential fixed costs grew by only ~2% in Q1 FY27 despite massive AUM expansion, proving that AI underwriting is decoupling overhead from AUM growth.
