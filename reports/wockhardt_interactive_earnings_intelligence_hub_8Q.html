EBITDA margins will briefly plateau around 21.0% - 21.4% during Q3-Q4 FY27. This is caused by management's decision to self-commercialise Zaynich in the US, absorbing upfront Medical Science Liaison and sales force expenditures prior to hospital formulary inclusion. Margins then expand rapidly to 28.48% by Q1 FY29E as >80% gross margin NCE sales eclipse fixed costs.
PAT growth outpaces EBITDA growth due to three structural tailwinds: (1) Extreme operating leverage; (2) Permanently lower finance costs following QIP debt repayment; and (3) A low cash tax rate (~11-14%) shielded by massive historical accumulated tax loss carryforwards.
The primary vulnerability is commercial execution in US hospital networks. Delays in hospital Pharmacy & Therapeutics (P&T) committee approvals or New Technology Add-on Payment (NTAP) coding delays could push the revenue adoption curve back by 6 months, causing unabsorbed SG&A cash burn.
