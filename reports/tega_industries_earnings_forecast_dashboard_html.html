📌 Core Investment Narrative

Tega Industries has executed a transformative leap by acquiring Molycop for USD 1.5 billion. This transaction provides Tega with dominant global market share across North America, Latin America, and Australasia in grinding media and comminution consumables.

Key Fundamental Catalysts:
  • Mine-to-Mill Integration: Combining Tega's DynaPrime composite liners with Molycop grinding balls.
  • High-Chrome Transition: Shifting Molycop from 20k to 200k tons capacity in premium High-Chrome media.
  • Chile Facility: $25M-$30M plant commissioning in Mar 2027 slashing LATAM freight costs.

🔍 Forensic Deconstruction of Q1 FY27

The headline net loss of ₹108 Crore in Q1 FY27 is an optical distortion created by standard M&A accounting treatment of exceptional acquisition costs.

Reported PAT (Q1 FY27) -₹108.0 Cr
(+) M&A Exceptional Charge +₹190.0 Cr (₹1.9B)
Adjusted Core Operating PAT ₹54.7 Cr (+26% YoY)

Legacy Tega standalone revenue grew 21% YoY to ₹430 Cr with EBITDA margins expanding to 24.1%.

⚠️ Leverage & Working Capital Watch

To fund the transaction, Tega assumed $672.5m in Molycop debt and issued a ₹1,500 Cr parent-level term loan, creating an quarterly interest burden of ₹180-210 Cr.

Primary Risk Factor: Working Capital

Tega operates with an extended cash conversion cycle exceeding 200 days and DSO of 100-114 days. If working capital bloats under Molycop, free cash flow conversion could stall.

Deleveraging Mitigant: ₹75.4 Cr from Apollo Funds' ₹95.4 Cr preferential issue is explicitly earmarked for immediate debt paydown.
