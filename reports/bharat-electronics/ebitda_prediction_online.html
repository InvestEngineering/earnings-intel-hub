⚡ Executive Summary & Inflection Thesis

Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited (BHEL) has entered a multi-year earnings inflection phase, catalyzed by a structural revival in domestic thermal power capacity additions (97 GW mandate by FY32), non-power diversification, and accelerating execution velocity. The current record order book of ₹2.39 lakh crore is transitioning from low-margin legacy fixed-price contracts to high-margin orders featuring price-variation clauses (PVC). Q1 FY27 marked a decisive break from historical Q1 operational losses, delivering ₹504 crore EBITDA and ₹377 crore PAT.
