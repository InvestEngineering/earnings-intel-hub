🟢 Top Bullish Themes Social Drivers

1. Unprecedented Order Book Expansion

Supported

BHEL secured ₹75,916 Cr inflows in FY26, bringing total backlog to ₹2.6 Lakh Cr in Q1 FY27 (7.2x revenue visibility).

Evidence: BSE filings confirm massive thermal boiler orders (NTPC, Adani Power) & Central Electricity Authority targets.

2. Structural Operational Turnaround

Supported

Q1 FY27 PAT of ₹376.71 Cr and positive EBITDA margin (6.55%) confirm operating leverage absorbing fixed overheads.

Evidence: Financial results show dramatic reversal from previous year's operating losses.

3. Coal Gasification & Nuclear Monopoly

Supported

Pivot to non-power heavy chemicals with ₹5,400 Cr BCGCL Coal-to-Ammonium Nitrate order and exclusive 700MW PHWR nuclear TG manufacturing.

Evidence: Cabinet approval of ₹37,500 Cr coal gasification scheme and official BHEL press release.

🔴 Top Bearish Themes Institutional Risks

1. Stretched Valuation vs Low ROE

Supported

60x TTM P/E and 5.58x P/B leave zero margin of safety for a capital goods business with sub-7% ROE and execution delays.

Evidence: Kotak, Jefferies, and Macquarie reports emphasizing multiple expansion outstripping EPS growth.

2. High Working Capital & Receivables

Supported

582 Gross Current Asset (GCA) days tie up liquidity; legacy power state discom receivables restrict free cash flow.

Evidence: CARE Credit Rating report & FY26 Balance Sheet disclosures.

3. Impending 2027 Board Vacuum

Supported

Simultaneous retirements of CMD K.S. Murthy, Director (Power), and Director (R&D) by Feb 2027 create leadership uncertainty.

Evidence: Public Enterprises Selection Board (PESB) tracking notifications.
