Key Value Catalysts

  • 1.
    Aggressive Branch Expansion: Management guidance to deploy 200 new branches in FY27, specifically scaling gold loan penetration in Northern and Western India.
  • 2.
    Liquidity Headroom: AGM approval secured for expanding overall borrowing limits to ₹23,000 Cr and issuing ₹2,500 Cr in NCDs to sustain >25% AUM compounding.
  • 3.
    Rating Upgrade Potential: CARE AA+ and IND AA+ ratings provide strong leverage; any positive outlook revision will compress borrowing costs (currently 7.80%).

Key Investment Risks

  • 1.
    Regulatory Oversight: Escalating RBI scrutiny on NBFC gold loan practices could enforce stricter LTV caps across loan cycles, impacting growth velocity.
  • 2.
    Product Concentration: Gold loans comprise 53% of total AUM. A sharp macroeconomic collapse in gold bullion prices could trigger LTV stress and margin calls.
  • 3.
    Floating Rate Liabilities: ~83% of borrowings are floating rate. A delayed domestic interest rate cut cycle could squeeze Net Interest Margins (NIMs).
