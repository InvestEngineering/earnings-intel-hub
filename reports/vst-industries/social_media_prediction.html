⚠️ Deep Mechanism: Price Sensitivity Trap

Compact tractor buyers are highly price-conscious marginal farmers. A 20% price hike cannot easily be passed through without triggering volume contraction.

Status: Fully Supported by Govt Drafts Impact: Margin Headwind
