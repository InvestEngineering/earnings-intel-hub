Executive Summary & 4-Quarter Earnings Intelligence

Borosil Renewables Ltd (NSE: BORORENEW) approaches H2 FY27 at a structural inflection point. After enduring severe predatory pricing in FY24–FY25, the company has stabilized operating EBITDA margins at peak historical levels (~34.8% to 35.1%). This thesis is supported by a comprehensive 5-year Countervailing Duty (CVD) on Malaysian imports issued June 2, 2026, existing Chinese/Vietnamese Anti-Dumping Duties (ADD), 93% captive renewable power integration, and an upcoming ₹950 Cr capacity expansion (+600 TPD) commissioning in late FY27.
