Key Upside Catalysts

  • 1. CGFMU Cash Realization: Expected receipt of ~₹65 Cr claim from CGFMU in Q3FY27 will prove safety-net efficacy in real-time.
  • 2. TVS Warrant Conversions: Strategic scaling of TVS stake to 9.9% over 18 months will continually fortify Tier-1 capital.

  • 4. Universal Banking License: Formal application progress with RBI will eliminate the "SFB valuation discount".

Key Downside Risks

  • 2. Promoter NCD Refinancing Friction: Dec 31, 2026 NCD repayment deadline at Jana Holdings could trigger temporary retail sentiment noise.
  • 3. Deposit Acquisition Competition: Systemic deposit scarcity in Indian banking may strain guided 23–25% deposit growth rates.
